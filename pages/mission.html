Our Mission


Barefoot shoes are catching on quick. There's a bunch of brands out there making cool designs for all kinds of activities, but not skateboarding for some reason. Some have a wide-ish toe, some have zero drop, most insoles have a bit of an arch. We're going back to the basics. Imagine you're standing barefoot on the ground. Your balance can align since you are flat and not on a downward slope. You use your arches which brings awareness back into your feet, and your toes can splay out and feel free.
